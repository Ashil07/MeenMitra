# MeenMitra — demo video script (4:00)

This is the final script. It includes the fixes from [demo_video_workflow.md](demo_video_workflow.md).
- **AI clip IDs** (0A, 1B…) point to the prompts in [ai_video_prompts.md](ai_video_prompts.md).
- **Screen clip IDs** (3A, 3B…) point to the run sheet in the workflow.
- **Narration** is about 540 words, which is about 3:50 at a calm pace. If you run long, drop the lines marked *(optional)*.

---

## Visual guidelines (whole video)

**Format**
- 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps.
- Keep text inside the middle 90% of the frame; judges may watch on a phone.

**Two looks, never mixed in one shot**
- **Story (AI clips):** cinematic and realistic.
  - Grade evenings warm orange, nights cool blue, mornings soft pink and gold.
  - Use slow camera moves only.
- **Product (screen recordings):** clean and steady.
  - No camera shake or tilted 3D screens.
  - Slow zoom-ins (110% → 130% over 3–4 seconds) to guide the eye.

**Moving from story to product**
- Fade through the phone screen: zoom into the phone in the AI clip, then cut to the full-screen app or chat mock-up.
- Elsewhere, use plain cuts or 0.3-second cross-fades. No flashy transitions.

**Highlights on the app**
- One rounded box or underline at a time.
- Danger items in red (#D64545), everything else in the app's teal.
- Draw it in over 0.3 seconds, hold 2 seconds, fade out.

**Cursor**
- Highlight it with a soft yellow halo.
- Move slowly and pause on each button before clicking.
- Hide it while you narrate over a still screen.

**Fonts**
- A single sans-serif (Inter, Poppins or Noto Sans) for all overlays.
- For Kannada, use **Noto Sans Kannada**; other fonts may show broken letters.

**Subtitles**
- Burn them in for every narrated line: white text on a 60% black box, bottom centre, at most two lines.
- For Kannada audio, put the English subtitle on a second line in yellow.

**Honesty labels** (keep these; they protect you with judges)
- **AI clips:**
  - Small "Illustrative scene" tag, top-left, at 70% opacity, on the opening and the WhatsApp story clips.
  - The call clips carry the disclaimer instead (see below).
- **Delivery disclaimer:** bottom-left, from 1:30 to 2:15:
  `Dramatization — WhatsApp and voice alerts are simulated in this MVP. No real messages or calls are sent.`
- **Concept tag:** on any planned feature, top-right:
  `Concept — planned feature`
- **Keep the app's own labels in frame:** "Demo data", "Simulated" and "Simulated (demo data)". Don't crop them out.

**Chat mock-up style**
- Use a plain green chat bubble design, no WhatsApp logo or name.
- The sender's name is "MeenMitra" with a fish icon.
- Add timestamps (5:00 PM) and read ticks.

**Music**
- One calm instrumental bed: soft at the start, a slight lift at 3:30.
- Duck it to −20 dB under the voice. Silence it under the phone call.

**Visual**
- **0A** (0:00–0:07): Ramesh feeding his pond at dusk.
- **0B** (0:07–0:13): the pond at night under heavy cloud.
- **0C** (0:13–0:20): morning; he stares at the water, worried.

**On screen:** "Illustrative scene" tag. At 0:16, centred white text fades in: *"What if you knew before sunrise?"*

**Narration**
> Imagine being a small fish farmer in coastal Karnataka. Your savings and your hard work are all in this pond.
> But at night, the oxygen in the water can fall dangerously low while you sleep. By morning, your fish may already be lost.
> What if you got a warning before that happened?

**Sound:** evening birds, then crickets at 0:07. A low, quiet music swell at 0:16.

## 0:40–1:10 · The evening forecast

**Visual**
- 0:40–0:52: **3B**. Choose **Normal night** and click **Run tonight's 5 PM forecast for all ponds**. The ponds tick to Safe one by one. Hold on "No oxygen alerts needed tonight."
- 0:52–1:10: **3C**. Choose **Cloudy, hot night** and run again. Kere 1 turns Danger; Hosa Kere and Temple Tank Pond turn Watch.

**On screen**
- At 0:44, top-right tag: *"5 PM — every evening"*.
- At 1:02, highlight the result line: *"3 of 6 ponds have a Watch or Danger oxygen forecast tonight."*
- Then highlight Kere 1's red **Danger** badge.

**Narration**
> Every evening at 5 PM, MeenMitra forecasts the night ahead for every pond.
> On a normal night, it finds no oxygen risk. No alerts needed.
> But what if tonight is cloudy and hot? With less sunlight, the algae make less oxygen during the day, and warm water holds less of it.
> Now three of the six ponds are flagged, and Kere 1 is at Danger.

**Sound:** a soft click sound on each run (optional).

**Narration**

**Visual**
- 1:30–1:36: **1A**. Ramesh with the feed sack; his phone buzzes.
- 1:36–1:44: **1B → chat mock-up**, full screen. The Kannada message appears, with the English version under it. Use the exact text from Clip 1 in the prompts file.
- 1:44–1:48: **1C**. He replies; a "1" bubble and the "ಧನ್ಯವಾದ!" reply appear. He pulls the feed sack away.
- 1:48–1:52: quick cut to **3E**: the Alert centre with the same message and the **Simulated · delivered** status.
- *(optional)* 1:52–1:58: **Clip 2**. He types a question. Show the **Concept — planned feature** tag.
- 1:58–2:03: **3A (call)**, 4:45 AM. The phone rings in the dark bedroom.
- 2:03–2:10: **3B (call)**. He listens. Kannada TTS audio plays with English subtitles in yellow.
- 2:10–2:15: **3C (call)**. 5 AM: he switches on the aerator and the water churns.

**On screen**
- Delivery disclaimer, bottom-left, for the whole segment.
- Time stamps top-right: *"5:00 PM"* at 1:30, *"4:45 AM"* at 1:58, *"5:00 AM"* at 2:10.

**Narration**
> At 5 PM, Ramesh gets the warning on WhatsApp, in Kannada, with English alongside. It tells him the risk, what's at stake, and exactly what to do. He replies "1" to confirm, and skips tonight's feed.
> *(optional)* In the full product, he'll also be able to ask questions, like what to do without an aerator.
> And because Kere 1 is at Danger, MeenMitra can also call him just before the aerator needs to start, when a text could easily be missed.

*Leave about 4 seconds with no narration from 2:04, under the call audio, so the Kannada voice is heard.*

- A notification "ding" at 1:31 and a soft sent sound at 1:45.
- Music off from 1:58. Ring, then the Kannada call with a phone-line effect, then a keypad beep.
- The aerator motor and splashing at 2:10.
- Crickets under the night shots.

**Narration**

## 3:05–3:30 · How it works

**Visual**
- 3:05–3:15: **3I**. Approach page. Click through the pipeline steps; end on "ML correction layer" with its *"Trained and tested…"* note.
- 3:15–3:30: **3J**. Data & models. Scroll down the water-quality model card to the predictions table, then to the oxygen prediction-vs-actual chart.

**On screen**
- Highlight **99.4%** at 3:17.
- Highlight the chart, where the model line tracks the actual line, at 3:23.
- Keep the *"Simulated (demo data)"* label visible.

**Narration**
> Under the hood, a physics-inspired model estimates overnight oxygen, combined with water-quality rules and farmer feedback.
> We've also trained two machine-learning models. One grades water quality with 99.4 percent accuracy on held-out data; the other cuts next-hour oxygen error by about 60 percent on simulated ponds.
> They won't change the risk score until we've tested them on real ponds.
